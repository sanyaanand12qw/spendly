Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you choose to add. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the features you signed up for.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard algorithms before being saved. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated expense records and personal information are permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) to operate the platform; these providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and may only process your data as needed to run the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
